A model of a Baja racing truck, featuring long travel live axle rear suspension, independent front suspension, positive caster on the front, and servo steering. This project was sponsored by BuWizz, a 3rd party controller manufacturer compatible with LEGO. The goal was a minimalist lightweight design to keep it fast and nimble 🛻
